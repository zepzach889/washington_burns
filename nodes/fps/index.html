Plan Your Federal Parks Visit
The Federal Parks Pass (‒65/year) grants unlimited access to all 57 Federal Parks and Reserves across the United States. Passes are available at all USRC stations and FPS visitor centres. All parks are accessible by USRC rail connection or FPS shuttle service.
🎫
Federal Parks Pass
‒65/year · All 57 parks · Transferable to immediate family · Available at all USRC stations
🚂
Rail Access
All parks within 20 km of a USRC station or FPS shuttle connection — no automobile required

Camping & Lodges

Accessibility
All visitor centres and primary trails are wheelchair accessible. Service animals welcome in all parks.
