Prime Ministers of Russia
Empire of Russia · From the Constitution of 1854 to the present · Reference list
Contents
  1. Overview
  2. List of Prime Ministers

This article lists the Prime Ministers of the Empire of Russia from the establishment of the office in 1855 through the present day. The office of Prime Minister was established by the Constitution of 1854. Under the original constitution, the Prime Minister was required to be drawn from the Chamber of Peers and was selected by the Chamber of Deputies. The reform settlement of 1908 removed the requirement that the Prime Minister come from the hereditary nobility and curtailed the tsar's power to freely appoint or dismiss the Prime Minister. The Duma Electoral Reform of 1929 established a regularised three-year electoral cycle, after which Prime Ministers' tenures became more closely tied to election results.

The earliest Prime Ministers served before formal party organisations had fully coalesced. Party affiliations are noted from the period in which they became meaningful. Under the three-year cycle established in 1929, if a Prime Minister dies or is removed by a Duma vote of no confidence between elections, their successor serves out the remainder of the current term before standing for election in their own right.
