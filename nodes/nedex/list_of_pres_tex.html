Presidents of the Texas Federation
List of executive heads of state and government since the 1844 Declaration.

The President of the Texas Federation is the head of state and head of government of the Texas Federation, elected to a six-year term with a two-term limit under the Texian Constitution of 1847. The office has been held by eleven individuals since the first presidential election in March 1848.

The two dominant political parties have been the Reform Party and the Democratic-Nationalist Party (DNP). The DNP has historically been the dominant governing party, with Reform winning the presidency during four administrations.

# Name Party Term Notes
1 Esteban Herrera Reform 1848–1854 First President. Hero of the Second Mexican War; unified the four founding states under a single federal banner.
2 Stephen Austin Democratic-Nationalist 1855–1866 Oversaw significant infrastructure projects. Abolished slavery in Austin state via compensated manumission. Served two terms.
3 Edward Marsh Democratic-Nationalist 1866–1872
4 James Whitmore Democratic-Nationalist 1872–1884 Oversaw the completiion of the Texas-California Trans-Continental Railroad in 1881. Served two terms.
5 Rodrigo Vásquez Reform 1884-1890 First Reform president in nearly 4 decades. Major labor reforms.
6 Samuel Reeves Democratic-Nationalist 1890-1902 In office during the time of the Voss Affair (though that would come to light in the Aldrich Administration). Served two terms.
7 Thomas Aldrich Democratic-Nationalist 1902-1908 In office during the USA's Caribbean War, and the Voss Affair.
8 Ernesto Salinas Reform 1908-1920 Served two terms.
9 Arthur Donnelly Democratic-Nationalist 1920-1923 Died in office on March 12, 1923
10 George Fallwell Democratic-Nationalist 1923-1932 Suceeded Donelly, then won his own term in 1926. In office during the founding of the North American Conference System.
11 Marcos Delgado Reform 1932-1940 President for the duration of the Global War. Served two terms.
