Revolution and Independence

The origins of the Federation lie in the collapse of the centralist Mexican government. Unlike the OTL (Our Time Line) Texas Revolution, this conflict saw a deeper integration of local Tejano leaders with Anglo settlers earlier in the process...

Federal Formation

Following the declaration of 1844, the four states of Austin, Brazos, Colorado, and Nuevo Mexico began the arduous task of drafting a shared constitution. The "Compromise of Texopolis" ensured that power remained decentralized...

"The Federation is not merely a treaty of states, but a promise of mutual defense against the encroaching Imperial shadows." — Esteban Herrera, First Inaugural Address
