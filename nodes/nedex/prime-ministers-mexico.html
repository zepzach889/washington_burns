Prime Ministers of the Empire of Mexico
From the Constitution of 1848 to the present · Reference list
Contents
  1. Overview
  2. List of Prime Ministers

The following is a list of the Prime Ministers of the Empire of Mexico from the establishment of the office under the Constitution of 1848 to the present. Under the 1848 constitution, prime ministers served at the pleasure of both the Emperor and the Cortes, with the Emperor retaining formal approval authority over the Cortes's nominee. This power was exercised most dramatically during the constitutional crisis of 1868–1869 under Raul II. By the reign of Jorge I, the imperial veto over prime ministerial appointments had become a dead letter in practice, and it was formally abolished by the Constitution of 1922, which established full parliamentary supremacy.

Under the Constitution of 1922, parliamentary elections are held on a fixed five-year cycle. If a Prime Minister dies or is removed by a vote of no confidence between elections, their successor serves out the remainder of the current term and may then stand for election in their own right.
