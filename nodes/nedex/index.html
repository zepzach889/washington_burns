Hugo Brandt
14th President of the United States · 1863–1869
Hugo Brandt (1812–1891) was the 14th President of the United States and before that Acting President of the Free State government during the War Between the States. He is regarded by historians as one of the most consequential figures in American history — the man who held the republic together during its greatest crisis, guided it through the abolition of slavery, and oversaw the constitutional convention that produced the Constitution of 1861.
