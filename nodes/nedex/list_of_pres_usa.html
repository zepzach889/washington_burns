Presidents of the United States
List of executive heads of state and government of the United States of America.

The President of the United States is the head of state and head of government of the United States of America. The president's official residence is Washington House, located in Franklin, District of Washington.

Since 1789, 44 individuals have held the office. For full details on the office itself — powers, election, the cabinet system — see President of the United States (Office).
