The Museum of the United States presents the story of the American republic across three floors of permanent galleries, the Great Hall of the Republic, and a changing program of special exhibitions. All galleries are included with free general admission.

Special Exhibition
Special Exhibition — Now Open
The Burning and the Building: Washington, August 1814
A landmark exhibition examining the destruction of the federal city on the Potomac and the extraordinary choices that followed — the debates, the journeys, and the founding of Franklin on the Ohio. Drawing on objects, maps, and personal accounts from the National Collection, the exhibition traces how a catastrophe became a founding moment. Highlights include original correspondence from the 1814 evacuation, surveyors' instruments and plats from the earliest plans for the new capital, and salvaged fragments recovered from the Potomac site.
Open through 30 November 2026  ·  West Wing, Level 2  ·  Free timed entry
Permanent Installation
Central Atrium · Ground Floor · Since 1879
The Great Hall of the Republic
At the heart of the Museum, beneath the great dome, the central atrium has welcomed visitors since opening day in 1879. The Great Hall is the permanent home of John Trumbull's most celebrated works of patriotic American art, displayed alongside items belonging to the early presidents and artifacts of the republic's founding era.
Declaration of Independence · John Trumbull Burning of the Federal City · John Trumbull Victory at Yorktown · John Trumbull
The Great Hall is open during all Museum hours. No timed entry required.

Upcoming Exhibition
Opening Autumn 2026 — Semiquincentennial
The Republic at 250
Marking two hundred and fifty years since the Declaration of Independence, the Museum's landmark semiquincentennial exhibition will bring together treasures from across the National Collection — from the founding generation to the present day — to tell the story of the American republic in a single sweeping installation. Full details and timed entry booking will be announced later this year.
Opening Autumn 2026  ·  Great Hall of the Republic & Level 2 Galleries
