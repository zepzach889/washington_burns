National Archives Gallery
Permanent Installation · Lower Level · Free Timed Entry
The Founding Documents of the United States
The Museum of the United States is the permanent home of the nation's three founding documents, held in climate-controlled preservation vaults and displayed in the National Archives Gallery. Visitors may view the original parchments under secure, controlled conditions.
Declaration of Independence · 1776 Constitution of the United States · 1787 Constitution of the United States · 1861
Timed entry passes required. Photography permitted without flash. Documents may occasionally be removed from display for conservation.

Special Exhibition — Now Open
The Burning and the Building: Washington, August 1814
A landmark exhibition examining the destruction of the federal city on the Potomac and the extraordinary choices that followed — the debates, the journeys, and the founding of Franklin on the Ohio. Drawing on objects, maps, and personal accounts from the National Collection, the exhibition traces how a catastrophe became a founding moment.
