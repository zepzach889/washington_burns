The Museum offers a year-round program of lectures, curator's talks, film screenings, and family events. All programs are free with general admission; evening lectures and screenings may require advance registration at the information desk.

Coming Up at the Museum
Recurring Programs
🏛️
Daily Highlights Tours
Every Open Day · 11 a.m. & 2 p.m.
A guided introduction to the Museum's treasures, beginning beneath the dome in the Great Hall of the Republic. No registration required — gather at the information desk.
🎓
The UUS Lecture Series
Monthly · Littlefield Auditorium
Presented jointly with the University of the United States, the Museum's flagship lecture series brings leading historians to Franklin to speak on the American past.
🎪
Family Days & First Fridays
First Friday · Open Until 9 p.m.
Hands-on family programming in the galleries each quarter, and extended evening hours on the first Friday of every month with music in the Great Hall.
The Semiquincentennial Year

Throughout 2026, the Museum's public programming marks two hundred and fifty years of American independence, building toward the autumn opening of The Republic at 250. Expanded lecture programming, special curator's talks, and commemorative events will be announced through the year.

Attending Events All events are free. Programs marked for the Littlefield Auditorium and evening screenings require registration at the information desk or by post. School group visits are arranged through the Museum's Learn & Research services.
