Education and scholarship have been at the heart of the Museum's mission since its founding — an institution created not merely to display the history of the republic, but to teach it and to advance its study. The Museum serves schoolchildren and senior scholars alike.

The University Partnership
Founding Partnership · Since 1879
The Museum & the University of the United States
The Museum stands directly across the street from the University of the United States, and the two institutions have worked hand in hand since the Museum's founding to promote historical research nationwide. UUS faculty serve as consulting curators, graduate students train in the Museum's departments, and the two institutions jointly appoint the Museum's annual research fellows.
Consulting Curators Graduate Training Annual Research Fellowships The UUS Lecture Series
Fellowship applications are reviewed jointly by the Museum and the UUS Department of History each winter.
For Schools & Families

The Museum welcomes school groups from across the Union throughout the academic year. School Group Days offer guided tours of the permanent galleries led by Museum educators, with programs matched to each level of study — from a first walk through the Great Hall to seminar-style sessions in the War Between the States gallery for older students. Teaching materials keyed to the national curriculum standards are available to schools on request.

For families visiting on their own, the Museum offers quarterly Family Days and hands-on gallery programming — see the Events page for the current schedule. Children are welcome in every gallery, every day.

For Researchers
📖
The Talbot Reading Room
By Appointment · Lower Level
Named for the Smithsonian Research Institute director who secured the Museum's founding, the reading room offers supervised study of documents and manuscripts from the National Collection.
🏺
Collection Access
By Advance Arrangement
Scholars may request examination of objects not currently on display, including holdings in the preservation vaults. Requests are reviewed by the relevant curatorial department.
🖼️
Images & Reproductions
For Study & Publication
Photographic reproductions of objects and documents in the National Collection are available for scholarly study and publication through the Museum's imaging service.
Fellowships & Publications

Each year the Museum appoints a class of research fellows — jointly selected with the University of the United States — who spend a year in residence working with the National Collection. The Museum also publishes The American Record, its scholarly journal of the history of the republic, in partnership with the UUS Press, alongside exhibition catalogues and gallery guides.

Arranging a Visit School group bookings, reading room appointments, and collection access requests are made through the information desk or by post. Reading room appointments require two weeks' notice; requests involving the preservation vaults require advance arrangement with the curatorial departments.
