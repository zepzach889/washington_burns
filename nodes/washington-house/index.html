The Four Wings

Washington House is a square structure enclosing a central courtyard, creating four distinct wings each with its own purpose.

North Wing — State
Ceremonial heart of Washington House. Home to the Presidential Study, the Reception Hall, and the formal offices of the presidency.
East Wing — Executive
The working wing. Houses the President's working office, the Cabinet Room, the Press Office, and senior staff. Not open to the public.
West Wing — Public & Diplomatic
Open to visitors and dignitaries. Contains the Ballroom, State Dining Room, Diplomatic Reception Room, and guest quarters for visiting heads of state.
South Wing — Private Residence
Private home of the First Family. Opens onto the south gardens. Not open to the public.
