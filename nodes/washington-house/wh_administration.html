The Nakamura Administration took office in February 2025. Under the Constitution of 1861, the officers of the cabinet are nominated by the President and confirmed by the House of Representatives, which retains the power to withdraw its consent from any cabinet officer. The cabinet is presided over by the Secretary General, a sitting member of the House chosen by that body — the constitutional bridge between Washington House and Congress.

Office of Communications

The Washington House Office of Communications conducts daily press briefings from the East Wing and administers press credentials for the Washington House press corps. Briefings are led by Press Secretary Delacroix. Transcripts of all briefings, presidential statements, and executive actions are published through the Briefing Room.

The Cabinet System
Under the Constitution of 1861, cabinet officers are nominated by the President and confirmed by the House of Representatives. The House may withdraw its consent from any cabinet officer, including the Secretary General, by majority vote. The Secretary General — a sitting House member chosen by the chamber — presides over the cabinet.
