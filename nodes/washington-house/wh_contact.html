Write to the President

The President welcomes correspondence from the public. Letters are received by the Office of Presidential Correspondence, which reads every letter sent to Washington House and prepares a selection for the President's desk. Owing to the volume of correspondence received, individual replies may take some weeks.

Offices & Enquiries
Visitor Office
Tour bookings, garden tours, school and group visits, and accessibility arrangements. See Plan a Visit for hours and guidance.
Office of Communications
Media enquiries, press credentials, and membership of the Washington House press corps. Briefing transcripts and statements are published in the Briefing Room.
Office of Scheduling & Advance
Invitations for the President or First Family to attend or address events. Requests should be submitted in writing at least eight weeks in advance.
Office of Presidential Properties
Enquiries concerning the building, the formal gardens, and the Union Gallery, including loans to and from the national art collection.
Public Records

Requests for records held by the Executive Office of the President under the Freedom of Information Act are administered by the Records Office. Requests should be made in writing to the Records Office at the Washington House postal address, identifying the records sought as specifically as possible. The Records Office acknowledges all requests and publishes frequently requested records for public reference.
