The Daily Briefing
Press briefings are conducted by Press Secretary Delacroix from the East Wing Briefing Room on most weekdays. Briefing times are posted each morning to the President's public schedule. Transcripts are published on this page following each briefing.
The Weekly Address
The President's Weekly Address is recorded each Friday in the North Wing communications suite and distributed nationally to broadcasters and the Interlink.

Press Credentials
Membership of the Washington House press corps is administered by the Office of Communications. Correspondents seeking day passes or permanent credentials may apply in writing to the Office of Communications, Washington House, 1 President's Square, Franklin, D.W.
