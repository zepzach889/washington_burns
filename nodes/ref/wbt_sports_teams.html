Baseball
National Baseball Association
NBA
The oldest professional league in WBT, consolidating from regional circuits in the 1910s. US-only. Championship: the Diamond Classic. Two conferences divided by the Mississippi River — Eastern (15 teams) and Western (15 teams). Detroit and Milwaukee are Canadian cities ITTL and do not appear in the NBA roster.

Football (Association Football)
North American Football Association
NAFA
The continent's premier football league, spanning five sovereign nations plus associate members. Championship: the American Cup. The American Cup champion earns automatic qualification to the WFA Global Cup. The WFA's Olympic tournament functions as the national-team world championship; the Global Cup is the club/franchise equivalent.

Stickball
All-Nation Stickball Association
ANSA
Rooted in the Native Nations of the Mississippi Valley — Gigadohi, Kanasaw, and Lakota — stickball is a US-only league with its cultural center in the interior South and Great Plains. "All-Nation" carries a double meaning: the nations of the US broadly, and specifically the Native Nations that anchor the sport's origins. Championship: the All-Nation Championship. Four divisions.

Major League Aroball
MLA

Rugby
Union Cup — Continental Rugby Championship
Union Cup
Unified during the Langston era (1971–1977) from three distinct traditions: the institutional/collegiate tradition, the Appalachian working-class tradition, and the Californian Commonwealth-immigrant tradition. Spans the USA, Union of the Californias, Canada, Hawaii, and the Federation of Texas. Governed globally by the newly founded World Rugby Council (WRC, est. 2026). Championship: the Union Cup.

Women's Professional Baseball
Women's Professional Baseball League
WPBL
Founded during the Global War era as men were drafted; integrated from day one. Nearly eliminated during the Nolan/Priest era; survived by the narrowest margin. By the Nakamura era (2025–2031) the WPBL has reached near-parity with the men's NBA — simply part of what American baseball means, without qualifier. US-only. Two conferences mirroring the NBA's East/West structure. Championship name TBD.
