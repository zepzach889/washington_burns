Nakamura Signals Compromise Path on Constitutional Convention
By Eleanor Voss, Political Correspondent · Franklin
President says she will not block Assembly vote if quorum conditions are met, breaking weeks of silence on the referendum question.

President Sophia Nakamura signalled late Thursday that she would not use executive discretion to block an Assembly vote on convening a constitutional convention, provided that the measure meets the quorum threshold required under the 1861 Constitution.

The statement, delivered through her Communications Director in a brief evening address from the Washington House, represents the first substantive break from weeks of presidential silence on what has become the defining domestic question of her term.

Federalist and SPA floor leaders hailed the signal as clearing the way for a floor vote as early as next month. Democrat and JCA leadership were more cautious, with JCA whip Senator Malone of Georgia calling the statement "deliberately ambiguous."

The convention question — whether to open the NAU's foundational compact to revision for the first time since 1912 — has split the Assembly along lines that do not neatly follow party affiliation.
