Washington Burns
An Alternate History  ·  Author's Note
◆
About this Project
◆

Washington Burns is an alternate history timeline with a single point of divergence: in August 1814, the destruction of Washington D.C. by British forces was more complete than in our timeline — and the consequences more far-reaching. The United States government relocated to Franklin, a new city on the Ohio River near Cincinnati, and American history diverged from there in ways large and small.

This site is a creative worldbuilding project simulating a small corner of that world's internet — its newspapers, broadcaster, encyclopaedia, government websites, and public institutions. Everything you encounter here is fiction set in an alternate 2026.

Washington Burns began as a forum project in 2017 and is currently being rewritten and expanded. The full timeline narrative is available to read at the link below.
