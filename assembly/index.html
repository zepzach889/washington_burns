Recent & Upcoming Legislation
S. 1142
North American Currency Stability Act
Would peg Canadian and Texan exchange rates to the Continental and establish a joint monetary oversight panel under NAU authority. Backed by a Federalist-SPA coalition.
On Floor Sponsors: Sen. Okafor (Fed–Ohio), Sen. Bertrand (SPA–Québec)
H.R. 887
Constitutional Convention Enabling Act
Would authorise the convening of a constitutional convention to review the 1861 NAU compact. Requires two-thirds quorum of member-state delegations. Presidential position clarified Thursday evening.
Rules Committee Sponsors: Rep. Halverson (Fed–Minnesota), Rep. Dumont (SPA–Montréal)
S. 1089
Federal Parks Expansion and Preservation Act
Designates eleven new Federal Park territories across the NAU, including three in northern Canada and one in the Hawaiian archipelago. Passed Senate committee 14–3.
Passed Committee Sponsor: Sen. Kahananui (Fed–Hawaii)
